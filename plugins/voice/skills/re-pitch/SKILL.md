The last reply did not land. Re-pitch it for a reader who has lost the
thread:

1. Restate where things are in two or three sentences — the goal, what is
   done, what is in flight — before anything else.
2. Make the failed reply's point again, conclusion first, in ASD-STE100
   Simplified Technical English word discipline: plain established words,
   one meaning per word, active voice.
3. Use only vocabulary the project's own docs define; define anything else
   in plain words on first use. Do not reuse a label, codename, or
   shorthand from earlier in the conversation without re-anchoring what it
   refers to.
4. Add no new information — this is a restatement, not a continuation.
